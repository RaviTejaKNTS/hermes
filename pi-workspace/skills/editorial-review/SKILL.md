# Editorial Review

## Review Checklist

- Does the opening make the reader care?
- Is the thesis clear?
- Are headings logically ordered?
- Are claims specific and supportable?
- Are examples concrete?
- Is any section repetitive or vague?
- Is the ending useful rather than abrupt?

## Response Shape

Lead with the highest-impact fixes. Then provide a revised version or patch-style edits if requested.
