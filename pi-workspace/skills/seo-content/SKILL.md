# SEO Content

## Workflow

1. Identify primary keyword, search intent, audience, and funnel stage.
2. Build a content brief with title options, meta description, headings, questions to answer, and internal-link opportunities.
3. Draft the article only after the brief is coherent.
4. Keep SEO natural. Do not keyword-stuff.

## Deliverables

When asked for an SEO article, produce:

- Primary keyword
- Search intent
- Suggested slug
- Title options
- Meta description
- Outline
- FAQ ideas
- Draft or next action

Save outputs under `/workspace/articles`.
